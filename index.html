SIMON SAY GAME

GAME RULES

  • Level 1: Watch the color blink, then click that exact same block.
  • Next Levels: Repeat all previous colors in order, then click the new flashing color last.
  • The Challenge: The chain grows by 1 new color every level. Memorize the whole sequence!
  • Game Over: Click the wrong color even once, and the game ends instantly.
